SOM101 under Prof. Mayank Pareek asked for a corporate case study. I chose Dell, mostly because its famous direct-to-customer, build-to-order model is a systems story: inventory as a liability, suppliers wired into demand, and a balance sheet shaped by negative cash-conversion cycles.

The analysis covered corporate structure after the EMC merger and the return to public markets, logistics and operations, the income statement, and market valuation. Presenting it to a class of more than 150 students was the first time I had to compress a large system into a story that held attention for twenty minutes.

It is a small piece of work, but the habit stuck: read the operating model before the numbers, and the numbers before the narrative.
